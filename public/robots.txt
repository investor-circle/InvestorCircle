# myinvestorcircle.com
#
# Public ideas and the stock pages that aggregate them are meant to be found.
# Member PROFILES are not: that was a deliberate decision, and the structural
# reason it holds is that profiles only exist as HashRouter routes
# (/#/investor/name), which are not separate URLs to a crawler at all. The
# rule below is the belt to that braces — if a profile ever does get a real
# URL, it stays out of the index until that decision is revisited.

User-agent: *
Allow: /

# Not indexable, and not to be crawled.
Disallow: /investor/
Disallow: /search

Sitemap: https://myinvestorcircle.com/sitemap.xml
